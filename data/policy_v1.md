# Alder & Co. Returns Policy (v1)

## 1. Return window
You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have the tags attached.

## 2. Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 business days of us receiving your return.

## 3. Return shipping
Return shipping is a flat $6.95, deducted from your refund. Return shipping is free for exchanges and for defective items.

## 4. Final sale
Items marked Final Sale, swimwear, intimates and gift cards cannot be returned.

## 5. Defective or damaged items
Report defective or damaged items within 90 days of delivery, with a photo. We cover the shipping. You can choose a refund or a replacement.

## 6. Exchanges
You can exchange for a different size or color within the return window, subject to stock. If the requested size or color is out of stock, we refund instead.

## 7. Gift returns
Gift returns with a gift receipt or order number are refunded as store credit at the price paid.

## 8. Store returns
Items bought online can be returned at any Alder & Co. store within the return window. Store returns have no return shipping fee.

## 9. International orders
International orders can be returned within 14 days of delivery. The customer pays return shipping. Duties and taxes are not refunded.

## 10. Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 7 days of purchase, you can get a one-time price adjustment. Price adjustments are not available on Final Sale items or on purchases made with promo codes.

## 11. Late or lost orders
If your order has not arrived within 10 business days of shipping, contact support. We open a carrier trace, then reship or refund.

## 12. Escalation to a human
Legal threats, chargebacks, fraud, and injuries must go to a human agent. Refunds over $300 need human approval.

## 13. How to start a return
Start a return in the returns portal. The portal gives you a prepaid label.
